The goal
A simulation of politics in Ishmael, with the parties and people as they are in September 2026. The goal: to stay in politics, to have influence and to carry out your policy.
The game ends if your party does not pass the threshold, if you are not elected to the Knesseton, if you are removed, if you are expelled from the party or if you retire. Losing an election does not end the game – you can continue in opposition.
Whom you play
Any of the real Knesseton members and candidates, or a character of your own who takes a place on the list.
The starting role follows the real role: prime minister, minister, party leader or Knesseton member. Each role has different powers.
How time passes
A regular turn is 4 months. In the 4 months before the election each turn is two weeks of campaign.
In each turn you decide as many decisions as you want. Each decision costs political capital (🎯), money or relations.
At the end of the turn the economy moves, the polls update, the enemies grow stronger and the politicians remember what you did. A detailed summary of the turn appears.
Coalition and negotiation
After the election the President tasks the person with the most recommendations with forming the government. There are 28 days, plus an extension of 14.
Each party demands portfolios, deputy ministers, committees, budgets, laws, vetoes and sometimes a rotation. You can give up demands or offer additions.
The agreement is written, and each commitment gets a deadline and a reminder. A violation may break up the government.
Legislation and values
Real laws go through a preliminary reading, committee and a third reading over several turns. Basic Laws need 61.
Each party votes according to its values. Some parties have red lines they will not cross even from inside the coalition.
Security and diplomacy
Six fronts, IDF units under their real names and operations approved by the cabinet. Each operation has a price: casualties, economy and foreign relations.
Alongside the military track there is a diplomatic track: dialogue channels, mediators, ceasefires, confidence-building steps, A/B/C areas and normalization.
Campaign and speeches
A party leader opens a campaign: strategy, target audiences, budget and slogan, and then house meetings, spots, debates, internal polls and surplus agreements.
The "🎤 Speech" button is always available: you choose a stage, subject, position and tone, and write or ask for a draft. Each party responds according to its values.
The adviser in the floating button gives a recommendation in every situation, and explains exactly what each button will do before you press.
It runs a simulation before it recommends, but the decision is yours.
Tips
Hovering the mouse over a button explains what it does. A "?" next to a number explains where it comes from.
The game saves automatically at the end of each turn, in your browser only.

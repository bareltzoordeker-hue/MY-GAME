About the game

A simulation of politics, from the campaign to the government.

The idea

Government of Ishmael is a political management game in the browser. It starts in September 2026, on the eve of the election, with the parties and candidates as they appear in the lists and polls. You play as any member of the Knesseton or candidate, or as a character of your own, and run a campaign, an election, a coalition, legislation, the economy, security and diplomacy.

What's inside

  • 16 parties and hundreds of politicians, each with values, relations and memory.
  • Coalition negotiations with a written agreement, deadlines and reminders.
  • Dozens of real laws and voting by values and red lines.
  • Economy, budget, public services and 25 population groups.
  • Security and diplomacy: fronts, IDF units, operations, dialogue and agreements.
  • Campaign, speeches and a relations map.

How it's built

The game is written in TypeScript and React and runs entirely in the browser, with no server. The simulation engine is separate from the interface and covered by automated tests. The caricatures and sound are generated in code.

Disclaimer

The game uses the names of real parties and public figures. The decisions, quotes, reactions and results in the game are a simulation only, and do not represent their positions, actions or intentions. The game is neutral and does not recommend voting for any party.

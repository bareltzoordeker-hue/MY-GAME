Accessibility statement

We want everyone to be able to use the site and the game.

Level of accessibility

The site has been adapted to the requirements of the Israeli Equal Rights for Persons with Disabilities Regulations (Service Accessibility Adjustments), 5773-2013, and to Israeli Standard SI 5568, which is based on WCAG 2.0 level AA.

What we did

  • Full keyboard navigation, including a "Skip to main content" link and a clear indication of the focused element.
  • Pop-up windows that can be closed with the Esc key, and focus moves to them when they open.
  • Screen-reader labels for buttons and icons, and alternative text for images.
  • Sufficient color contrast in texts.
  • Text enlargement up to 200% without the page breaking, and a layout adapted to phone and computer.
  • Respect for the operating system's "reduce motion" setting – animations stop.
  • Accessibility menu (the ♿ button on every page): text enlargement, high contrast and stopping animations.
  • The font is loaded from the site itself, and the language and direction are set on every page.

Known limitations

  • The game itself is a complex simulation with a lot of data, tables and charts. Some charts are also shown as numbers in text, but the screen-reader experience may not be complete on every screen.
  • The caricatures are decorative illustrations. The name and role of each character always appear in text next to them.
  • Google ads (outside the game) come from a third party, and we have no full control over their accessibility.

We keep improving. If you ran into a problem – we'd like to know and fix it.

Accessibility coordinator

The creators of Government of Ishmael · email: balkeryyyy@gmail.com. So that we can handle it, state in your message the page, what you tried to do, what the problem was, and which browser and device you used. We'll try to answer within 5 working days.

Tested browsers

Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari in their current versions, on computer and phone.

Areas A, B and C: what the Oslo Accords set

A short explanation of how Judea and Samaria were divided into three kinds of area, and who is responsible for what in each.

Where it comes from

In the Oslo II interim agreement (1995), the areas of Judea and Samaria were divided into three kinds, in order to hand powers over gradually to the Palestinian Authority. The arrangement was meant to be temporary, but has remained in force in practice for many years.

The three areas

In Area A, about 18% of the land, civil and security responsibility lies with the Palestinian Authority. In Area B, about 22%, civil responsibility is Palestinian and security responsibility is Israeli. In Area C, about 60%, Israel is responsible for everything, and it includes the settlements and most of the open land.

Why it is disputed

Area C matters to the Palestinians as room to develop, and to Israel for security and settlement reasons. Moving land from C to B or from B to A is a charged diplomatic move that weakens or strengthens coalitions, and affects foreign relations.

How it works in the game

On the security and diplomacy screen you can move land in the diplomatic direction (to B or to A) or the opposite way, and change relations with the Authority, the US and neighboring countries. Every such move costs political capital and affects the coalition.

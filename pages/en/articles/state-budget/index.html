The state budget: what a deficit is and why it matters

How a budget is built, where the money comes from, and what happens when the state spends more than it collects.

Income and spending

The state's income comes mainly from taxes: income tax, VAT, corporate taxes and more. Spending is what the government hands out to ministries: education, health, security, welfare, transport and so on. Every shekel given to one place does not reach another.

What a deficit is

When spending is higher than income a deficit is created, and the state borrows to cover it. The money it owes is called debt. As debt grows, more interest has to be paid on it, which cuts the money left for services.

Tools for change

The government can raise taxes, cut spending, or encourage growth so that income grows. Each tool has a price: high taxes hurt the wallet, cuts hurt services, and growth takes time. A budget is not just numbers but a choice of who receives.

How it works in the game

On the budget screen you divide money between ministries and change taxes. Every change affects the deficit, the quality of services and the population groups. To pass the budget you need a majority in the Knesset, and losing the vote brings elections closer.

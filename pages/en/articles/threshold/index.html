The electoral threshold and why every vote counts

How votes become seats, what the electoral threshold is and why small parties fight over it.

From votes to seats

In national elections you vote for a party list and not for a person. The 120 seats are divided according to the percentage of the votes each list received. A list that gets about a quarter of the valid votes gets about a quarter of the seats.

The electoral threshold

A list that does not pass a certain percentage of the valid votes does not enter the Knesset at all, and its votes disappear. The threshold in Israel is 3.25%, about four seats. Its purpose is to prevent a multitude of tiny parties and to support stability.

What it does to parties

Parties around the line merge, fight for the votes of undecided voters and sometimes try to draw voters away from partners. A vote for a party that will not pass counts as a wasted vote, so polls that show it close to the line change how voters behave.

How it works in the game

In Government of Ishmael, if your party does not pass the threshold the game is over. That is why in the campaign you will have to choose target audiences, a budget and a slogan, and follow the polls until election night.

A bug, an idea, a question or a request – we'd love to hear it.

How to reach us

By email: balkeryyyy@gmail.com. We'll try to answer within a few working days.

Content removal requests

If you believe content on the site or in the game harms you or violates your rights, write to us at the same address with the subject "Content removal request", and specify what the content is, where it appears and why it harms you. We will review every request promptly and remove or correct content where justified.

Accessibility

Ran into an accessibility problem? The details are in the accessibility statement, or directly by email above.

Before you write

  • Game disappeared? The save is in your browser. Clearing the site's data, private browsing or a different device – and the game won't appear. On the "Save and settings" screen you can export the game to a file.
  • No sound? Browsers only play sound after a first click. On a phone – check that the mute switch is off.

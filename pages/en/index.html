What the game is

Politics, as one system

Government of Ishmael is a management game: every decision affects the economy, the public, your partners and your rivals.

🗳️

Parties and people

16 parties and hundreds of members of the Knesseton and candidates, based on the lists and polls of September 2026. Every party has values, demands and red lines.

🎯

The goal

Stay in politics and make a difference: get through the election, form a government or lead the opposition, and carry out your policy.

⚖️

Neutral

The game takes no position and recommends no party. The decisions, quotes and results are a simulation and do not represent the views of real people.

How to play

Four stages

  1. Choose a politician

    Any member of the Knesseton or candidate, or a character of your own who takes a place on the list. You start in the real person's actual role: prime minister, minister, party leader or Knesseton member.

  2. Campaign

    Four months before the election, each turn is two weeks: strategy, target audiences, speeches, debates, internal polls and surplus-vote agreements.

  3. Election and coalition

    Election night, recommendations to the president and 28 days to reach 61: portfolios, committees, budgets, laws, vetoes and rotation – and a written agreement with deadlines.

  4. Running the country

    Two-month turns: budget, legislation, ministries, security and diplomacy. Every turn ends with a detailed summary of what happened.

What's in the game

Depth at every layer

🤝

Coalition negotiations

Portfolios, deputy ministers, committee chairs, budgets, laws, vetoes and rotation. A written agreement with deadlines, reminders and a price for breaking it.

📜

Legislation by values

Dozens of real laws. Every party votes by its values, and there are red lines that are not crossed even from inside the coalition.

🛡️

Security and diplomacy

Operations named after the real units, casualties and international reaction – alongside dialogue, mediators, confidence-building steps and normalization.

🎤

Campaign and speeches

Campaign strategy, speeches with an automatic draft, debates and polls. Every party reacts according to its values.

📈

Economy and services

A 13-item budget, taxes, deficit and debt, public services and 25 population groups that react to every decision.

♿

Accessible and open to all

No sign-up and no installation, with an accessibility menu, keyboard navigation and high contrast.

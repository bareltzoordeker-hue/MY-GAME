Privacy policy

What information is stored, where, and what you can do with it.

Who we are

The site memsheletismael.netlify.app and the game "Government of Ishmael" are operated by the creators of Government of Ishmael ("we"). For any privacy question: balkeryyyy@gmail.com.

What we collect

The site has no sign-up, no user accounts and no server that receives information from the game. The game runs entirely in your browser, and we do not collect or store personal information about you.

What is stored in your browser

The game stores the following in your browser's localStorage, on your device only. The information is not sent to us:

  • The saved game (hakise.autosave.v1) – saved automatically at the end of every turn.
  • Sound and music settings (hakise.sound) and accessibility settings (hakise.a11y).
  • Whether you have already seen the tutorial and the opening notice (hakise.tutorial.done, hakise.disclaimer.v2).
  • Your cookie choice (hakise.consent.v1).

You can delete all of this at any time by clearing the site's data in your browser.

Ads and cookies (Google AdSense)

The site shows ads through Google AdSense. Third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies and identifiers to serve and measure ads, including personalized ads based on your previous visits to this and other sites.

  • Consent: a cookie notice appears on your first visit. Without your consent, only non-personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time through the "Cookie settings" link at the bottom of every page.
  • You can opt out of personalized advertising in Google's Ads Settings, or opt out of third-party vendors' cookies through aboutads.info.
  • More information: How Google uses information from sites that use its services.

The site has no cookies of our own and no analytics tool.

External services

  • Google AdSense – as detailed above. Google may process information on servers outside Israel.
  • Netlify – hosting of the site. Like any server, it receives your IP address and browser details in order to serve the pages.

The site's font is loaded from the site itself, not from external servers.

Your rights

Under the Protection of Privacy Law, and for visitors from the European Union also under the GDPR, you have the right to access information about you, correct it, delete it and object to its processing. Since we do not store information about you, most of these rights can be exercised directly in your browser (deleting the site's data) and in Google's settings. For any other request: balkeryyyy@gmail.com.

Children

The site is not intended for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information about them. The content deals with politics and security and is suitable for teenagers and adults.

Changes

We will update the policy when something changes, and update the date below. Last updated: October 7, 2026.

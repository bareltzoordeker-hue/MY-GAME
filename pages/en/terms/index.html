Simulation, satire and the use of real names

"Government of Ishmael" is a simulation and political satire game. The game uses the names of real parties and public figures and well-known public events in order to create a simulation of the political system. "Ishmael" and "Knesseton" are fictional names.

The decisions, quotes, reactions, events and results in the game are produced by the simulation engine and the player's choices. They do not represent the positions, actions or intentions of the people and parties, and do not attribute to them any act, trait or intention. The caricatures are generic illustrations generated in code.

The content is not intended to harm any person, community or sector. Sensitive events, such as casualties in security operations, are presented in a factual and respectful tone.

Political neutrality

The game takes no political position, is not election propaganda and does not recommend voting for any party or candidate. All parties and positions receive equal treatment in the game.
